# Generic guidance changes for round three

The frozen round-two guidance already asked for research, a complete first draft and diagnostic rereading. This round adds a short teaching-focused section and makes the second pass more operational:

- First-use local terms need a purpose, not only a definition or acronym expansion.
- Abstract relationships may need one small concrete instance and an explanation of what to notice.
- Explanatory continuity must survive beyond the opening chapters.
- Review notes distinguish comprehension gaps from technical corrections.
- Mentally execute important examples, proportionally.
- After revision, reconcile the whole document so changed passages, decisions and examples tell one story.

No rag-workbench-specific facts, corrected APIs or previous reviewer answers are added to the skill. No prescribed word count or mandatory subsection template. Installed skills and prior fixtures remain unchanged.

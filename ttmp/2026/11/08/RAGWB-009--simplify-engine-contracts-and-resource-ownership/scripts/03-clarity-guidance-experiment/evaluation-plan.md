# Round-three evaluation fixed before launch

Primary criterion: each guide teaches a capable newcomer clearly across its entire length. Read all five complete finals. Assess orientation and first-use local terms; concrete relationships/examples; connected explanations and transitions; sustained clarity in the middle/end; usefulness and proportionality of detail. Judge a common quality floor, not merely a winning sample. No aggregate numerical score or model ranking.

For each writer, record at least two strengths and two concrete remaining comprehension burdens with actual passages/sections. Compare preserved first draft, review notes and final to identify substantive explanatory changes. Minor naming or source precision corrections must not dominate the writing verdict. Separate a bounded technical review; flag contradictions that make behavior hard to understand and departures from original requirements. Do not demand complete implementation specifications to recognize good explanation.

Compare with round two, particularly first-use terminology, concrete instances of abstract relationships, explanatory continuity through later chapters, and agreement across examples/decisions/prose. Preserve source and proposal distinctions. Inspect material source claims, but do not repeat exhaustive code audit or broad tests for a docs-only experiment.

Inputs, model families and four-artifact procedure stay equivalent to round two. Writers do not see prior guides, reviews, or this rubric. No control arm, blind evaluator or human-intern test exists; claim observed artifact differences, not isolated causal effects. Stop after reporting; do not install the skill or replace the original guide automatically.
